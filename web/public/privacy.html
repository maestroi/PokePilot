Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

RomPilot (rompilot.app) is a personal project in which an AI plays Game Boy games. Visitors can watch live runs and replays.

What we collect

We have no accounts, no advertising and no third-party analytics. Our web servers may keep standard request logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for a short time, for security and to keep the service running.

Google and YouTube data

RomPilot uses the YouTube Data API only to upload gameplay videos it has rendered to the project owner's own YouTube channel. It requests only the youtube.upload scope. It does not read, store or share data about any other YouTube or Google user. The OAuth credentials are kept on the server and are never shown to visitors. RomPilot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploaded videos are covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke RomPilot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
